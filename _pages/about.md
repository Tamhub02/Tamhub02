I am an AI Engineer at Airamatrix working on computer vision and deep learning systems for digital pathology. My work focuses on Whole Slide Image (WSI) processing, defect segmentation, vision-language models, and high-performance inference pipelines.

### Focus Areas

- **Histopathology Defect Segmentation**: Training ConvNeXt-based UNet models to identify slide scanning and preparation artifacts under extreme class imbalance using custom focal and dice loss formulations.
- **WSI Streaming & Reconstruction**: Building multithreaded tile streaming and coordinate-space stitching pipelines for 50GB+ pyramidal TIFF and SVS slides.
- **Vision-Language Models**: Adapting multimodal models (such as Qwen2.5-VL) with LoRA and structured reasoning workflows for pathology evaluation.
- **Object Detection Benchmarking**: Evaluating detection architectures (Faster R-CNN, RetinaNet, RTMDet, YOLOX) on cellular targets using MMDetection.
- **Anomaly Detection**: Extracting patch embeddings and using k-NN metrics for out-of-distribution tissue detection.
